# GellSoft RentPro: user manual

RentPro keeps the bookings of a rental shop: wedding costumes, party decor, equipment, vehicles. Everything is saved on your phone and works without internet.

## First start
Enter the shop name, what you rent, your phone and address. You can change them later in Settings.

## The screens
- **Home**: money collected this month, money still to collect, what goes out and comes back today, and what is overdue. The eye button hides the amounts. Under it are shortcuts to every part of the app.
- **Bottom bar**: Home, Bookings, **+** (new booking), Items, Customers.
- **Menu** (top left): Reminders, Invoices, Reports, Backup and restore, Billing and GST, Account, GellSoft Pro, Settings.

## Items
Add what you rent: name, category, size, **quantity**, rent (per event or per day), deposit. Rent and deposit are for **one piece**. With GST on, each item also has a GST rate and a SAC/HSN code (rental of goods is 9973). An item can be marked **In repair** (it cannot be booked) or **Retired**.

**Quantity**: a shop with ten chairs adds one item "Chair" with a quantity of 10. A booking takes as many as it needs (the **- 2 +** control on the booking), and the app counts the pieces that are still free on every day: eight chairs booked for a wedding leave two for anyone else on those days.

**Category**: choose from the list made for your kind of shop (costume, equipment, decor, vehicle, makeup), or choose **Type my own category** and write one. Categories you have typed are offered again next time.

## Makeup artist
Choose **Makeup artist** as what your shop does (Settings, Shop details, or when adding a shop). The app then speaks of **Services** instead of items and a booking is one **appointment**: a date and a time, the services (with a quantity, for example 4 bridesmaids), the advance and the balance. There is nothing to pick up or return, and two clients can be booked for the same service on the same day. When the appointment is over, tap **Mark as done**. The reminders, WhatsApp messages and invoices are worded for appointments.

## Customers
Name, phone, address and an ID note (for example "Aadhaar ending 1234": the ID itself is never stored). With GST on you can add the customer's GSTIN and state.

## Booking
1. Tap **+**. Choose the customer and the items.
2. Set the **event**, **pickup** and **return** dates. The app refuses an item that is already booked for any of those days and says who has it.
3. Add a discount and the advance paid. The balance, GST and deposit are shown as you type.
4. On the booking page you can take payments (advance, balance), take the **deposit**, mark it **picked up**, and mark it **returned**. When you mark it returned the app asks how much deposit to give back (keep some back for damage, with a reason).
5. A booking can be edited until it is returned, and cancelled until it is picked up.

Day-rate items are charged for every day from pickup to return, both included.

## Photos
On a booking, tap **Add photo** to take a picture or **choose from the gallery**, where you can **tick as many photos as you like** at once. Each is marked pickup, return or other. They are kept in the app, not in your gallery. The free plan keeps 2 photos on a booking; Pro keeps any number.

## Billing and GST
Open **Billing and GST** and switch **Charge GST** on. Enter your GSTIN and state, choose whether rents already include GST, and the letters for invoice numbers (for example RNT gives RNT-0001).

On a booking tap **Create tax invoice** (or **Create bill** when GST is off). The invoice shows CGST and SGST when the customer is in your state and IGST when not. You can share it as a PDF, print it, or send a message on WhatsApp. The deposit is not part of the invoice because it is returned. An invoice can be cancelled and replaced; its number is never used again.

Every payment has a **receipt** (PDF or WhatsApp).

## WhatsApp
On a booking: send the booking details, remind the customer to collect the items, to return them, or about the balance. In **Reminders**, each line has a WhatsApp button with the message ready. RentPro opens WhatsApp; you press send.

## Reminders
**Reminders** lists what needs doing: items to send (from the number of days before the event that you set on each booking), items due back, overdue returns and money to collect.

With **Pro**, the phone also shows a **morning summary** and a **reminder before each event** at the time you choose (Settings). Pro can set **several reminder times a day** (up to four, for example 8 AM, 1 PM and 6 PM) with **Add a reminder time**. The first time you open the app it asks to show notifications; if you said no, turn it on in the phone's settings for RentPro.

## Brand logo
In **Settings, Shop details**, choose your **logo**. It is printed at the top of your invoices and receipts, and shown on Home and in the menu.

## Several shops
With **Pro** you can run more than one shop on one phone (for example a costume shop and a makeup studio). Tap the shop name on Home, or **Your shops** in the menu or Settings, to switch, and **Add a shop** to make another. Each shop keeps its own items, customers, bookings, invoices, GST and invoice numbers, logo and staff logins, so nothing mixes. Press and hold a shop in the list to delete it. Backups include every shop. Reminders from every shop arrive on the phone, with the shop name in front.

## Staff logins
In **Settings, Staff logins** (Pro) give each person their own PIN and a role. Everyone signs in before using the app, and each booking shows who made it.

- **Admin**: everything, including settings, backup, reports, GST files, cancelling and managing people.
- **Staff**: bookings, payments, discounts, items, customers and reports. No GST files, settings or cancelling.
- **Cashier**: bookings and taking payments only.

When you turn logins on, the app shows an 8-digit **recovery code once**: write it down, it is the only way back in if the Admin forgets the PIN. Each shop has its own people. Staff logins stay on the phone and are not part of a backup. If Pro ends, everyone can still sign in; only adding or changing people needs Pro.

## Reports and export
**Reports** shows rent billed, collected, still to collect, deposits held, GST, and the top items and customers for today, this month, last month, this financial year or a range you pick. The share button exports spreadsheets (CSV): bookings, payments, the sales register with GST, GSTR-1 B2B, B2C and HSN sheets for your accountant, customers and items.

## Backup
**Backup and restore** saves everything, with the photos, to one file, or to a private folder in your own Google Drive (sign in with Google first). Restore on a new phone by choosing the file or restoring from Drive. Pro can back up to Drive every day by itself.

## Free and Pro
Free: 12 new bookings every month and 2 photos on each booking, with everything else. Pro (Rs. 99 a month or Rs. 999 a year): unlimited bookings and photos, reminder notifications at several times a day, the daily Drive backup, more than one shop, and staff logins. Nothing you made is ever locked: you can always view and export it.
